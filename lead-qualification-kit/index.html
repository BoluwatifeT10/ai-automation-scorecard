How to use this kit

This edition is written for a digital agency qualifying prospective clients. The blanks can be reused for a second offer later. Finish the pages in order. A later page does not repair an unclear definition of a qualified lead.

What you will have when you finish

  • One sentence that states what “qualified” means for this offer.
  • Must-have signals, secondary signals, and automatic disqualifiers.
  • Ten questions in your words, each marked required or optional.
  • A minimum fee, a floor, a decision window, and three score cutoffs.
  • A prompt you can paste into the agent, with brackets filled in.
  • The CRM fields the workflow must write.
  • Four messages that send only when consent and channel allow it.
  • Six test results and a go-live decision.

Work in this order

Scope of this edition. Use it on new business inquiries: a form, a chat, or an email to the agency. One offer. One CRM. One reviewer, plus a backup named on page 4. A person still owns Priority replies, every escalation, and any lead the CRM did not store.

Rules that keep the system honest

  1. Set the minimum, the floor, and the decision window before any live lead is scored.
  2. Score only words the prospect used. If a 0, 1, or 2 definition does not match, leave that factor blank and escalate.
  3. Apply the category rules in the printed order. Stop at the first match.
  4. Send nothing when inquiry consent is No. Send a nurture message only when later-contact consent is Yes.
  5. Use only a channel the prospect chose. Do not move email consent over to phone or SMS.

Lead qualification framework

The workflow makes four decisions. Each decision is scored from a stated answer, then the category rules on page 8 choose the next action.

Fit

The prospect is a client this offer can serve. Compare what they said with the must-have and secondary signals on page 4.

Intent

They named a business problem and, for a top score, asked for a concrete next step. Curiosity without a problem is not intent.

Timing

They gave a decision date or a start date. Compare that date with the decision window on page 4.

Authority

You know whether this person can approve the fee, or who can, and whether that person is already involved.

Value

They stated a number or a range. Compare it with the minimum and the floor. Do not convert currency. Do not guess a budget.

Next action

Priority, Qualified, Nurture, Disqualify, or Escalate. Escalate is a hold for a person. It is not a fifth sales status.

One definition, shared. The qualified-means sentence on page 4 is the sentence sales and marketing both use. The agent does not invent a softer one. The agent also does not quote a fee, promise a result, book a contract, or take payment. A person does that after Priority or Qualified.

What the agent is for

The agent collects missing answers, scores them with the printed definitions, and returns a structured record. The workflow around the agent checks consent, writes the CRM, sends at most one template, and alerts the reviewer when the record says Escalate or the write fails.

Ten qualification questions

Use these wordings, or rewrite them on page 6. Do not add an eleventh question to the default agent. Seven are required by default because a score depends on them. Three are optional context.

# Question, what it proves, and the pass signal Default Feeds
1 What outcome do you want an agency to deliver? Proves there is a job to do. Pass: a concrete outcome, such as pipeline, launches, or a channel to be run. RequiredFit, Intent
2 What is in place today? Gives the reviewer the current setup. It does not change the total by itself. OptionalContext
3 What changed that made you look for an agency now? Separates an active problem from browsing. RequiredIntent
4 Which brand, product, or business is this for? Checks company type and market against page 4. RequiredFit
5 What have you already tried, including other agencies? Flags a request for unpaid speculative work. If they never describe that request, do not assume it. OptionalIntent
6 When do you need a decision or a start? Pass for Timing 2: a date on or before the last day of the decision window. RequiredTiming
7 What budget or fee range is approved or under discussion? Pass: a number or a range in the currency written on page 4. “Flexible” is not a number. RequiredValue
8 Who can approve the engagement? Pass for Authority 2: the speaker is that person, or that person is already on the thread or booked. RequiredAuthority
9 What constraints apply to platform, market, or contract type? Checks the remaining must-have and secondary signals. RequiredFit
10 What would make the first 90 days a success? Gives the reviewer a line to repeat in the reply. It does not change the total. OptionalContext

Answers that are too vague to score

Ask that question once more, in different words. If the second answer is still on this list, leave the factor blank and escalate. Do not translate a vague answer into a 1.

Not an outcome. “Grow,” “help us,” or “ideas.”

Not a change. “Just looking,” with no event in the business.

Not a business. A first name, and no brand or market.

Not a date. “Soon,” “ASAP,” or “sometime.”

Not a fee. “Flexible,” “depends,” or “market rate.”

Not a person. “The team,” “we,” or “management,” with no name.

Lead qualification starter kitScoring definitions

Page 07

What 0, 1, and 2 mean

Use the definition that matches the prospect’s words. Do not pick the nearer score. If none match, leave the factor blank and escalate. Total = Fit + Intent + Timing + Authority + Value. Maximum 10. A blank factor means there is no total.

Fit

From questions 1, 4, and 9, compared with page 4. If the secondary list is blank, Fit is only 2 or 0.

2 Every must-have signal matches. No automatic disqualifier is present.

1 Every must-have matches except exactly one signal, and that signal is on the secondary list. No disqualifier.

0 Any must-have fails, or any automatic disqualifier is present.

Intent

From questions 1, 3, and 5. A discovery call they asked for is a next step. An unpaid pitch, unpaid creative, or a guarantee of results is speculative work.

2 They named the business problem and asked for a call, proposal, audit, or pricing.

1 They named the business problem and did not ask for a next step.

0 They did not name a business problem, or they asked only for unpaid speculative work.

Timing

From question 6, compared with the decision window on page 4. “Next quarter” is a time reference. “Sometime” is not.

2 They stated a decision date or start date on or before the last day of the window.

1 They stated a month, quarter, or date that falls after the window.

0 They gave no time reference, or they said they are not deciding and not starting.

Authority

From question 8. “I will recommend someone” is a 1 only if they name that person.

2 The speaker is the budget holder, or the budget holder is copied or already booked.

1 They name the budget holder and their own role in the recommendation. That person is not involved yet.

0 They cannot name the budget holder, or they have no role in the decision.

Value

From question 7, compared with the minimum and the floor. No number means 0. Do not treat “flexible” as a range.

2 A number or range is stated, and its low end is greater than or equal to the minimum.

1 A number or range is stated, the low end is below the minimum, and the high end is at or above the floor.

0 No number or range is stated, or the high end is below the floor.

Priority, Qualified, Nurture, Disqualify

Walk the rules from 1 to 9. Stop at the first match. Escalate means a person decides. It is not a category you email.

  1. Disqualify. Inquiry consent is No.
  2. Disqualify. Any automatic disqualifier is present, or Fit is 0.
  3. Escalate. Any required question is unanswered after two asks, or any factor is blank.
  4. Disqualify. Value is 0.
  5. Priority. Fit is 2, Intent is 2, Timing is 2, Authority is 1 or 2, Value is 2, and the total is at least the Priority minimum.
  6. Qualified. Fit, Intent, Timing, and Value are each 1 or 2, Authority is 1 or 2, and the total is at least the Qualified minimum.
  7. Escalate. The total equals the review score, or Authority is 0 while Fit is 2 and Value is 1 or 2.
  8. Nurture. Fit is 1 or 2, and later-contact consent is Yes.
  9. Escalate. Later-contact consent is not Yes.

Priority

Rule 5 only. The prospect asked for a next step, can start inside the window, meets every must-have, and is at or above the minimum.

Send the Priority template on an allowed channel. The reviewer replies inside the window on page 4.

Qualified

Rule 6 only. They match the offer well enough to speak with, and they miss at least one Priority condition.

Send the Qualified template. A person runs the next conversation. The agent does not book the work.

Nurture

Rule 8 only. They fit the offer, and an earlier rule did not fire. Typical case: the date is outside the window, or the total is below the Qualified minimum and is not the review score.

Send the Nurture template only. One message now. A later message only on the date you recorded, and only while later-contact consent stays Yes.

Disqualify

Rules 1, 2, or 4. No consent, a failed fit or a named disqualifier, or no usable budget figure.

Rule 1: send nothing. Rules 2 and 4: send the Not a fit template once, and only if inquiry consent is Yes. Do not add a sequence.

Defaults assume a maximum of 10. If you change a number, use the same number in the prompt. Do not lower the Priority minimum below the Qualified minimum.

Worked record. Scores 2, 2, 2, 1, 2. Total 9. Rules 1–4 do not match. Rule 5 matches. Category Priority. Send the Priority template. A second lead scoring 2, 1, 1, 1, 1 totals 6. Rules 1–6 do not match. Rule 7 matches. Escalate. Send nothing.

Agent prompt

Replace every bracket with the worksheet values. Paste from START through END on page 10. Do not paste the examples.

Start of prompt

Role

You qualify prospective clients for [AGENCY_NAME]. The only offer you qualify is [OFFER]. You collect missing answers, score them, and return one CRM record. You are not the salesperson. You do not close, price, or book work.

Responsibilities

  • Read the inquiry, including any answers the form already stored.
  • If inquiry consent is No, stop. Set category Disqualify. Send no question.
  • If a page-4 disqualifier is already explicit, stop asking. Score Fit 0 and follow the category rules.
  • Ask required questions that are still blank, one at a time, in numeric order.
  • Score Fit, Intent, Timing, Authority, and Value only with the definitions below.
  • Assign the category by walking the rules in order and stopping at the first match.
  • Return the record in the output format. Do not add fields.

Conversation rules

  • Tone: [TONE]. Default: plain, specific, and calm. No urgency and no slang.
  • Ask one question per turn. Do not ask a question the prospect already answered.
  • Required questions are those marked required on the question worksheet. If that worksheet is blank, required questions are 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 9.
  • Use the worksheet wording when it is filled in. Otherwise use the default wording below.
  • Accept an answer only when it is specific enough to score. If it is not, ask that question once more, in different words. Two asks is the maximum.
  • If they say they do not want contact, stop. Set consent_inquiry to No.
  • Do not ask for a channel they already declined. Record the channel they chose.

Default questions

  1. What outcome do you want an agency to deliver?
  2. What is in place today? Optional.
  3. What changed that made you look for an agency now?
  4. Which brand, product, or business is this for?
  5. What have you already tried, including other agencies? Optional.
  6. When do you need a decision or a start?
  7. What budget or fee range is approved or under discussion?
  8. Who can approve the engagement?
  9. What constraints apply to platform, market, or contract type?
  10. What would make the first 90 days a success? Optional.

Values from the worksheet

Decision window: [DECISION_WINDOW_DAYS] days from the day the inquiry arrives. Minimum: [MINIMUM_VALUE]. Floor: [VALUE_FLOOR]. Must-haves: [MUST_HAVES]. Secondary: [SECONDARY]. Disqualifiers: [DISQUALIFIERS]. Priority minimum: [PRIORITY_MIN]. Qualified minimum: [QUALIFIED_MIN]. Review score: [REVIEW_SCORE]. Reviewer: [REVIEWER_NAME].

Scoring

Fit 2: every must-have matches and no disqualifier is present. Fit 1: exactly one secondary miss and no disqualifier. Fit 0: any must-have fails or any disqualifier is present. If Secondary is blank, do not use Fit 1.

Intent 2: they named the problem and asked for a call, proposal, audit, or pricing. Intent 1: they named the problem and did not ask for a next step. Intent 0: no problem named, or the only ask is unpaid speculative work.

Timing 2: a decision or start date on or before the last day of the window. Timing 1: a month, quarter, or date after the window. Timing 0: no time reference, or they are not deciding and not starting.

Authority 2: the speaker is the budget holder, or that person is copied or booked. Authority 1: they name the budget holder and their own role, and that person is not involved yet. Authority 0: they cannot name the budget holder, or they have no role.

Value 2: a number or range is stated and the low end is at or above the minimum. Value 1: a number or range is stated, the low end is below the minimum, and the high end is at or above the floor. Value 0: no number or range, or the high end is below the floor. “Flexible” is not a number. Do not convert currency.

If a definition does not match, leave the factor blank. Do not choose the closer score. These are too vague to score: “grow,” “just looking,” “soon,” “flexible,” and “the team.” Total is the sum of five scores. A blank factor means no total.
